I took these photos while travelling from Pakistan to Switzerland. I flew through Emirates and had to change my flight at Dubai (this allowed me to take some closeups of the metropolis).

The flight route included these countries:

Since we took off from Dubai, I could take some closeups of the metropolis.

{% set gallery4 = [

We flew above the Persian gulf on our first strand of the journey. I briefly saw the port of Qatar, before flying close to the seashore of Saudi Arabia. I tried hard to locate the seashore of Iran, but was only able to see a faint glimpse before it disappeared.

  ["/photos/middle-east/IMG_8183.webp","/photos/middle-east/IMG_8190.webp"]

I saw quite a few of these on the shore of Saudi Arabia. They might have something to do with water desalination.

{% set gallery6 = [
  ["/photos/middle-east/IMG_8200.webp"]

{% gallery gallery6, "Middle East from Above" %}

Shortly after we entered the airspace of Kuwait. The massive bridge below is called the Sheikh Jaber Al-Ahmad Al-Sabah Causeway which connects Kuwait city with the outer region. It is the fourth longest road bridge in the world with a length of 36.14 km.

{% set gallery7 = [
  ["/photos/middle-east/IMG_8211.webp","/photos/middle-east/IMG_8215.webp"]
] %}
{% gallery gallery7, "Middle East from Above" %}

The thin line below is the Kuwait-Iraq border. It was quite strange to see the contrast between a desert and a (somewhat) developed region.

  ["/photos/middle-east/IMG_8219.webp"]
