Les Ateliers de Marinette is a vintage shop located in the city of Lyon. The road besides the shop leads to the <a href="https://adl1995.github.io/wanderings/lyon-basilica-botanique-jardin.html">Basilique Notre-Dame de Fourvière</a> and overlooks the Saône river.
