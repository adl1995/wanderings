Although the park was quite big, it only contained a single wild animal: a Guldar Cheetah. The board below say that this Cheetah is found in all fours provinces of Pakistan and is known to prefer solidarity. It also prefers walking on narrow paths due to which it gets hunted easily. It is known for attacking its preys swiftly.
