Girona is a major Catalan city located in the northeastern part of Barcelona. It is known for its medieval architecture and the Roman remains of a fortress. It is a popular day trip from Barcelona. I also considered visiting the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvador_Dal%C3%AD">Salvador Dalí</a> museum, which is located in a nearby city named Figueres, but couldn’t do so due to time constraints.
